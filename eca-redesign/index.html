Matthew Morrison Presents Show Up

A gleeful, genre-crossing journey of music, movement, and meaning with a charming and familiar star of stage and screen.

This event has been rescheduled from March 29, 2026.

Matthew Morrison, known for his standout role as Will Schuester on “Glee,” is a dynamic performer whose charisma and versatility have captivated audiences for years. With a background in musical theater, Morrison effortlessly blends his impressive vocal talents with a strong acting presence, bringing depth and charm to his performances. In his brand-new show, Matthew Morrison Presents Show Up, step into a world where music and dance become more than just entertainment—they become a journey into the heart of what makes us human.
