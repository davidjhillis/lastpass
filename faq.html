AI Monitoring & Protect

Included in LastPass Business Max.

LastPass AI Monitoring discovers the AI tools your team reaches through the LastPass browser extension, including tools IT didn’t approve. It also shows whether each one is accessed with SSO, a password or unvaulted credentials.

Discovered apps, login methods, credential vault status and user-level activity appear in your Admin Console dashboard. It runs in the browser your team already uses, so there is no new agent to deploy.

LastPass AI Protect warns employees in the browser when they are about to share credentials, API keys or personal information with an AI tool. Every warning is logged in Admin Console reporting, so you have a record of AI data risk across your organization.

AI Protect doesn’t read or log the full content of AI conversations. It logs only the warning, not the data that triggered it.

It can, so AI Protect lets admins set a rule for each AI tool rather than an all-or-nothing switch: allow approved tools, warn employees before they use tools you’re still evaluating, and block tools that don’t fit your AI strategy.

AI Monitoring lets you tag tools as approved or unapproved, so you can match those rules to how your team actually works.

Start with visibility, then add guidance. AI Monitoring shows which AI tools your team uses, including unauthorized ones, and how they log in. AI Protect then adds a real-time warning before sensitive data is shared, and lets you warn about or block tools that don’t fit your strategy.

Both run through the LastPass extension your team already has. There are no agents to deploy and no employee training is needed.

The main risk is a visibility gap: without a view of which AI tools are in use, IT can’t see unauthorized tools, how employees log in to them, or where credentials may be exposed.

The second risk is that credentials, API keys or personal information are shared with an AI tool by mistake. AI Monitoring closes the first gap, and AI Protect warns employees at the moment of risk.

LastPass VPN and LastPass Plus

LastPass VPN is included in LastPass Plus.

LastPass Plus includes both LastPass Premium and LastPass VPN for $84 a year, or $7 a month.

Many people prefer password protection and private browsing from the same provider because it simplifies account management.

LastPass VPN runs in its own app and uses the LastPass login you already have. There are no new accounts to create, and it works in the background, separate from your vault.

A no-log policy means the VPN provider doesn’t keep records of your browsing activity. LastPass VPN is a no-log VPN.

A VPN helps protect your data on public Wi-Fi in airports, hotels and cafés. With LastPass VPN you get password security and browsing privacy with one login and one subscription.

LastPass VPN provides fast, private browsing from almost anywhere, and its no-log posture keeps your connections confidential wherever you work.

Billing and trials

Charges, payments and ending a trial.

This can happen when automatic renewal is on and you also bought a subscription during your current billing cycle. The new purchase doesn’t replace the existing subscription, so the renewal adds a second charge. Contact LastPass Support and choose Account and Billing as the subject.
